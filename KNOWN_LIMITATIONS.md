# HeartAI Known Limitations & Non-Clinical Disclosures

**Project**: HeartAI — Cardiovascular Clinical Intelligence Platform  
**Model Architecture**: Calibrated Logistic Regression (Isotonic Calibration, 5-Fold Stratified CV)  
**Operating Threshold**: T = 0.09  
**Regulatory Status**: **INVESTIGATIONAL RESEARCH PROTOTYPE ONLY**  

---

> [!CAUTION]
> **NOT A MEDICAL DEVICE**:
> HeartAI has NOT been cleared, certified, or approved by the US FDA, European Medicines Agency (CE mark), or India CDSCO.
> It MUST NOT be used for primary diagnosis, patient triage, clinical prescription, medication adjustment, or emergency medical dispatch.

## 1. Epidemiological & Dataset Limitations

### 1.1 Cross-Sectional Survey Source (CDC BRFSS 2023)
The machine learning model was trained and evaluated on 397,870 usable records from the 2023 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System (BRFSS) survey conducted by the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).
- **Self-Reported Data**: BRFSS collects data via landline and cellular telephone interviews. Features such as hypertension, diabetes, smoking habits, and physical activity are self-reported and subject to recall bias and social desirability bias.
- **Cross-Sectional Design**: The dataset captures participants' reported health status at a single point in time. It measures **association**, not prospective biological causality.
- **Output Definition**: Model probability represents the *estimated statistical probability that a respondent with a matching demographic and risk profile reported a history of cardiovascular disease (`_MICHD`)*. **It does NOT represent the probability of suffering a future heart attack.**

### 1.2 Target Leakage Exclusion
To maintain scientific validity, direct diagnostic questions (such as `CVDINFR4` — *Ever told you had a heart attack?*, and `CVDCRHD4` — *Ever told you had angina or coronary heart disease?*) were strictly excluded from predictor features. Including them would artificially inflate accuracy to near 100% while rendering the model clinically useless.

### 1.3 Survey Sampling Weights (`_LLCPWT`)
In clinical practice, population surveys employ multi-stage probability sampling weights (`_LLCPWT`) to produce state- and nation-wide prevalence estimates. Individual single-record predictions generated by HeartAI do not incorporate these weights, representing unweighted individual sample estimates.

## 2. Feature Vector Constraints & Symptom Exclusion

### 2.1 Fixed 25-Feature Whitelist
The machine learning model operates strictly upon the 25 validated demographic, behavioral, and clinical indicator features defined in the CDC BRFSS codebook.
- **Symptoms Are Strictly Excluded from ML Inputs**: Acute symptoms (such as active chest pain, shortness of breath, nausea, or dizziness) are **NEVER** injected into the 25 feature slots.
- Symptom assessment is handled exclusively in the separate generative AI triage layer (`/api/v1/ai/symptoms`), ensuring the epidemiological ML model is never corrupted with acute presentation data.

---

## 3. Emergency Keyword Detection Boundaries

### 3.1 Deterministic Pre-LLM Regex Boundaries
Acute cardiovascular and neurological emergencies are screened deterministically before invoking Gemini AI to prevent LLM latency delays or hallucinations:
- **Scope**: The regex patterns detect classic emergency descriptors (e.g. crushing chest pain, radiating pain, elephant on chest, severe breathlessness, speech slurring, collapse).
- **Limitation**: Natural language descriptions of medical emergencies can vary widely. Novel metaphors, slang, or uncommon linguistic phrasings may not match the regex triggers.
- **Mitigation**: HeartAI mandates that all AI responses include prominent disclaimers and emergency hotline information (**112 / 102 in India**, 911 in US, 999 in UK).

## 4. Google Gemini API Status & Fallback Behavior

### 4.1 Verification Status in the Absence of API Credentials
- If `GEMINI_API_KEY` is not present in the runtime environment:
  - Generative AI features operate in a **Safe Educational Fallback Mode** with deterministic educational templates.
  - Live Gemini generation is recorded as **NOT VERIFIED (Unconfigured API Key)** in verification audits.
  - The API status endpoint (`/api/v1/ai/status`) transparently reports `configured: false` with instructions for authorizing access via Google AI Studio.

## 5. Architectural & Concurrency Constraints

### 5.1 In-Memory vs. Distributed Rate Limiting
- By default, SlowAPI operates in-memory (`memory://`).
- In multi-worker deployments (e.g. Gunicorn with multiple Uvicorn worker processes), in-memory rate limiting tracks limits per worker rather than globally across the cluster.
- **Remedy**: Multi-worker production environments must configure `REDIS_URL` to enforce a unified global rate limit across all worker processes.

### 5.2 SQLite Concurrency Lock
- SQLite is supported strictly for single-user local development and test automation.
- In multi-user concurrent environments, SQLite can experience `database is locked` errors during concurrent writes.
- **Remedy**: Production deployments must configure `DATABASE_URL` with PostgreSQL.
